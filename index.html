From Arcadia Page

"As I've been writing about identity, productivity, and purpose over the years, I've discovered that it's easy to create a life that looks right but doesn't fit who you truly are. Most ideas on being productive and creating a meaningful lifestyle don't consider individuality. I believe that you can experiment your way into finding what methods of getting things done, self-care, and simply being that are the best for the way you navigate the world. I created this space to share my personal remedies for creating an aligned life through introspection, self-care, fiction, and dreams, while making it possible for you to discover ways to bring more of your authentic self to your life."
