Over the years, I've written many books and articles for INFPs. As an INFP, many of my struggles have centered around the challenges of managing my extraverted intuition (Ne) and the endless flow of ideas that comes from that and managing my inferior extraverted thinking (Te), which creates tension surrounding being productive and organized in the external world.

Also as an INFP, I'm connected to my deep inner emotional landscape via the relationship between introverted feeling (Fi) and the imaginative extraverted intuition (Ne). So my journey as an INFP has also been about understanding my emotional life better, especially as I experience difficulties and change.

Since I've created so many things about my experience as an INFP over the years, I'm collecting my favorite things here for easy exploration.

My most popular articles and books for INFPs are at the top of this list, but pick whatever calls to you the most and start there:
